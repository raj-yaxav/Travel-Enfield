Leh with Nubra & Pangong (5N 6D)
About Leh with Nubra Pangong

A Leh to Leh tour with Nubra & Pangong (5N 6D) is one of the best ways to experience the raw beauty of Ladakh. This carefully planned journey takes you through high mountain passes, dramatic landscapes, and peaceful monasteries, offering a perfect balance of adventure and sightseeing. Starting and ending in Leh, the trip allows enough time for acclimatization while covering Ladakh’s most iconic destinations.

The highlight of this Leh Ladakh Nubra Pangong trip is the scenic drive to Nubra Valley via Khardung La, one of the world’s highest motorable roads. Nubra’s sand dunes, monasteries, and unique terrain create a completely different experience. Another unforgettable part of the itinerary is Pangong Lake, famous for its changing shades of blue and serene surroundings.

Ideal for couples, friends, and families, a Leh to Leh Nubra Pangong tour package offers comfortable stays, smooth transfers, and well-paced travel days. Whether you’re planning a short Himalayan escape or a first-time Ladakh adventure, this Ladakh tour package 5N 6D ensures you witness the region’s highlights without rushing. With breathtaking views at every turn, this journey promises memories that last a lifetime.
